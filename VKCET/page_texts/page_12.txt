1.5 Objectives
The main objectives identified in the reference material are:
• Build agentic AI architectures and frameworks that can change and grow.
• Allow agents to operate independently while cooperating toward common goals.
• Study how reinforcement learning and collaborative multi-agent learning can improve
decision-making.
• Improve scalability, flexibility and smooth operation as the number of agents increases.
• Improve real-time task delegation using agent capability, availability, resources and system
goals.
• Examine practical use in healthcare, manufacturing and logistics.
• Study the effect of Agentic AI frameworks on accuracy, productivity and operational cost.
1.6 Organization of the Report
Chapter 1 introduces the topic, motivation, scope and objectives. Chapter 2 reviews relevant
research and identifies the research gap. Chapter3 explains the system architecture and
methodology. Chapter4 describes the workflow and implementation-oriented operation.
Chapter5 presents the performance measures an reported evaluation. Chapter 6 discusses
results, advantages, applications and limitations. Chapter 7 describes future scope, followed by
the conclusion, references and appendices.
