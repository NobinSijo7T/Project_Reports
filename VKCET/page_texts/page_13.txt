LITERATURE SURVEY
2.1 Overview
Research in Multi-Agent Systems has developed across robotics, distributed systems, business
process management, healthcare and other domains. Earlier systems often used static task
allocation in which tasks were assigned to agents without much ability to modify the
workflow. The reference paper identifies this lack of flexibility as a motivation for more
adaptive multi-agent models.
The literature discussed in the reference paper covers logic-based multi-agent systems, agent-
based programming, dynamic task allocation, cooperation, learning mechanisms, task
decomposition, coordination and modern Agentic AI. These areas provide the conceptual
foundation for autonomous workflows.
2.2 Multi-Agent Systems
Calegarietal. (2021) is identified as a systematic literature review on logic-based technologies
for multi-agent systems. The relevance to this seminar is the broader understanding of how
multiple agents can be coordinated as components of a distributed intelligent system.
The key idea carried into MAWS is that agents can have separate responsibilities while still
participating in a common coordinated process.
2.3 Agent-Based Programming
Cardoso and Ferrando (2021) review agent-based programming for multi-agent systems.
Agent-based approaches provide a conceptual basis for representing autonomous entities, their
behavior and interactions.
