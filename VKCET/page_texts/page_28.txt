6.4 Healthcare Applications
In healthcare, multiple agents can support patient and resource management. Agents may
coordinate information, resources and task priorities. The reference paper reports healthcare as
one of the scenarios used in its evaluation.
6.5 Manufacturing Applications
In manufacturing, agents can support production task management and coordination.
Manufacturing workflows may contain several dependent tasks, making task decomposition
and resource-aware allocation useful.
6.6 Logistics Applications
Logistics workflows can involve delivery tasks, routes, schedules and changing resource
availability. Agents can cooperate to manage these activities and adapt when conditions such
as route changes affect the workflow.
6.7 Autonomous Vehicles
The reference paper identifies autonomous driving and changing situations as an application
area. Multiple agents can support decision-making and coordination in environments where
conditions change in real time.
