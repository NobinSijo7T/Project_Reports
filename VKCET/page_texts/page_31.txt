7.6 Autonomous Vehicles
The reference paper identifies self-driving vehicles as a future application. Multi-agent
coordination could support decision-making in changing traffic and operational conditions.
7.7 Wider Industrial Use
The approach can be extended to additional industries and smart environments where tasks are
interdependent, resources are shared and conditions change over time.
7.8 Human-AI Collaboration
Future systems can combine autonomy with controlled human oversight. Instead of completely
removing humans, the system can allow people to supervise important decisions, handle
exceptional cases and intervene when the agents are uncertain.
