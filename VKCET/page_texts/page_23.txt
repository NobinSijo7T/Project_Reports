4.4 Security and Human Oversight Considerations
Autonomous systems should be designed with appropriate control because agents may make
decisions that affect resources or workflows. The reference material explicitly notes that
human supervision may still be required in some situations.
A practical system should therefore support monitoring, logging, controlled permissions and
the ability to intervene when a workflow reaches a sensitive or uncertain state. These are
implementation considerations rather than new claims from the reference experiments.
