6.8 Smart Environments
Future-oriented applications includes mart environments and smart cities, where multiple
intelligent services may need to coordinate information and resources. The multi-agent
approach is suitable for such settings because the environment can contain many independent
but related activities.
6.9 Limitations
The reference material identifies several limitations:
6.9.1 Managing many agents becomes difficult as system size increases.
6.9.2 Communication becomes more complex when more agents interact.
6.9.3 Larger systems require additional computing resources
6.9.4 Complex workflows need stronger coordination mechanisms.
Some situations may still require human supervision.
These limitations show that autonomy and scalability introduce engineering challenges. A
successful MAWS must therefore balance agent independence with system-level control and
coordination.
