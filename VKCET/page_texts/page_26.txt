5.7 Reliability and Recovery
The reference paper reports recovery time and reliability for failure scenarios. Agent failure is
reported with a recovery time of 2 minutes and system reliability of 98%. Resource failure is
reported with 3 minutes and 97% reliability, while task overload is reported with 4 minutes
and 96% reliability.
These values indicate that the paper considered the ability of the system to continue operating
when a component or resource becomes unavailable.

5.8 Evaluation Limitations
The supplied reference material does not provide complete details of experimental hardware,
datasets, statistical significance testing or a formal mathematical definition of every
performance. Therefore, the reported values should be understood as the values presented in
the reference paper rather than independently verified experimental results for this seminar.
