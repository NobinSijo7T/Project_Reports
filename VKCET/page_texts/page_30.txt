CHAPTER 7
FUTURE SCOPE
7.1 Improved Agent Communication
Future systems can improve the reliability, speed and efficiency of communication among
agents. Better protocols can reduce unnecessary messages and help agents exchange only the
information needed for a decision.
7.2 Intelligent Decision-Making
More advanced decision-making mechanisms can help agents select better actions when
several alternatives are available. The reference paper identifies learning-based approaches,
including deep reinforcement learning, as an area for further development.
7.3 Larger-Scale Agent Coordination
Future MAWS can support larger numbers of agents and more complicated workflows. This
will require better coordination algorithms, resource management and scalable communication
mechanisms.
7.4 Improved Learning
Agents can be improved through learning from previous actions and feedback. Collaborative
learning can also allow agents to use information gained through interaction with other agents.
7.5 Smart Cities
Smart-city environments contain many services such as transport, resource management and
coordinated public services. Multi-agent workflows could allow these services to operate as
cooperating intelligent components.
