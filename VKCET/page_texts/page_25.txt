5.4 System Efficiency
Reported efficiency increases from 80% with 5 agents to 90% with 20 agents. Within the
presented test, this is associated with better workflow performance as work is distributed
among more agents.
The supplied material does not provide a precise mathematical definition of the efficiency
metric, so this report does not invent one.
5.5 Scenario-Based Evaluation
The reference paper also reports results for healthcare, manufacturing, logistics and dynamic
task-assignment scenarios. The healthcare workflow is reported with 8 agents, 3.5 hours
average task completion time and 90% resource utilization. Manufacturing is reported with 10
agents, 5.2 hours and 85% resource utilization. Logistics is reported with 12 agents, 6.1 hours
and 80% resource utilization. Dynamic task assignment is reported with 15 agents, 4.9 hours
and 83% resource utilization.

5.6 Collaboration and Adaptability
The reference paper reports collaboration efficiency of 92% for healthcare, 88% for
manufacturing, 85% for logistics and 90% for dynamic task assignment. Adaptability time is
reported as 5, 7, 6 and 4 minutes respectively.
These results indicate that the reference evaluation considered not only completion time but
also how well agents cooperate and how quickly the workflow can respond to changes.
