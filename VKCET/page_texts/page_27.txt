CHAPTER 6
RESULTS, DISCUSSION, ADVANTAGES AND APPLICATIONS
6.1 Results Summary
The overall reported results indicate that MAWS can improve workflow handling when
multiple agents cooperate. The agent-count test associates an increase from 5 to 20 agents with
a reduction in completion time from 7.3 to 4.2 hours and an increase in reported efficiency
from 80% to 90%.
The scenario-based results also show that the system was evaluated across different workflow
characteristics. Healthcare produced the highest reported collaboration efficiency among the
listed scenarios, while logistics had a lower collaboration efficiency because route and
timetable changes create dependencies.
6.2 Discussion
The main benefit of the architecture is not simply the number of agents but the distribution and
coordination of work. A complex task can be decomposed into manageable units, specialized
agents can work on those units, and information can be exchanged between them.

agents create more communication and coordination overhead. Therefore, scalability requires
careful system design rather than simply increasing the agent count.
