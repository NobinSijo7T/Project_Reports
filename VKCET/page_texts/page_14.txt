2.4 Dynamic Task Allocation
Grosset et al. (2024) is associated in the reference material with dynamic task allocation in
autonomous industrial vehicle fleets and V2X cooperation. Dynamic allocation is directly
relevant to MAWS because task assignments may need to change according to workload, agent
availability and changing requirements. A flexible allocation mechanism prevents a workflow
from remaining tied to an unsuitable assignment after the environment changes.
2.5 Agent Cooperation
Mu et al. (2024) surveys cooperation among agents, humans and agents in social dilemmas.
Cooperation is important to MAWS because multiple agents may need to exchange
information and coordinate their actions to achieve a shared goal.
The reference paper also discusses collaboration as a mechanism for sharing knowledge,
resources and planning information.
2.6 Agentic AI
Acharya et al. (2025) provides a recent survey context for Agentic AI and autonomous
intelligence for complex goals. The reference paper uses Agentic AI frameworks as a basis for
making agents more autonomous, adaptive and capable of decision-making.
