    \enspace\enspace Function $C$ links to $A$ and $B$ because $C$'s parameters require their output variables.\\
    \enspace\enspace $A$ and $B$ share no edge because neither depends on the other's intermediate state.\\[3pt]
    $\bullet$\enspace\textbf{Execution Schedule (Topological Concurrency):}\\
    \enspace\enspace Tasks with $\text{in-degree} = 0$ ($A$ and $B$) execute \textbf{concurrently in parallel worker threads}.\\
    \enspace\enspace When $A$ and $B$ complete, $C$'s in-degree drops to $0 \implies C$ runs $\implies D$ runs.%
